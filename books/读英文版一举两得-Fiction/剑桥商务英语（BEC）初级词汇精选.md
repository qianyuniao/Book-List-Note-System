### Day 01

abandon a claim 放弃索赔 / abandon property 放弃财产

abbreviation[əˌbriːviˈeɪʃn]n. 缩写；缩写词，缩略语

business correspondence to make it more brief and efficient. 商务信函中通常使用

abbreviation of/for sth. …的缩写

abide[əˈbaɪd]vt. 忍受，容忍 vi. 持续；停留；遵守

abide by the law 遵守法律 / abide by the contract 履行合同

innovative ability 创新能力 / marketing ability 营销能力 / managing ability 管理能力

abnormal[æbˈnɔːml]adj. 反常的，异常的；不规则的

abolish[əˈbɒlɪʃ]vt. 取消，彻底废除(制度等)；完全破坏

abolish poverty 消除贫困 / abolish personal income tax 取消个人所得税

abound [əˈbaʊnd] vi. 富足，多产；大量存在

abound with/in sth. 有大量…的；富于…的

abundant supply of cheap labour 廉价劳动力供应充足)

travel abroad 出国旅游 / at home and abroad 国内外

absentee [ˌæbsənˈtiː] n. 缺席者，缺勤者 adj. 缺席者的；在外者的；在外地主(或业主)的

absentee bid 委托竞买

absenteeism n. (经常无故的)旷工；缺席

absolute advantage in competition 竞争中的绝对优势

absolute majority (选举)绝对多数 / absolute power/authority 绝对权力/权威

absorb large losses 承受巨大的损失 / absorb talents 引进人才

academician n. 院士；学会会员

商 Internet access 互联网接入 / market access 市场准入

accession n. 增加；增加物；就职

accident insurance 意外保险

by accident 偶然地

accommodate oneself to sth. 自我调整适应… / accommodate demands of special interests 照顾到特殊利益集团的要求

accommodation n. 住所，住处；贷款，融通

reach/sign a currency accord 达成/签署一项货币协议

keep accounts 记账

account for 说明；占…比率；报…的账 / on account of sb./sth. 由于… / take account of sth. 考虑到…

certified/registered accountants 注册会计师，执业会计师

accrue[əˈkruː]vt. 获得；积累 vi. 产生；自然增长，(尤指利息)增加

accrue to sb. from sth. …来自…的逐步增长

accumulate a fortune 积累财富

accurate budget 精确的预算 / accurate market orientation 准确的市场定位

acknowledgement of receipt 回执

acquire a good reputation 获得好的声誉 / acquire success 获得成功

Insolvency Act《破产法》

acting manager 代理经理 / acting CEO 代理首席执行官

adapt to the development trend of world economy 适应世界经济的发展趋势

adaptor n. 适配器；多功能插头

add insult to injury 

additional contract/clauses/tax 附加合同/条款/税 /

address economic disputes 解决经济纠纷

adjourn[əˈdʒɜːn]vt. 推迟；使…中止 vi. 延期；休会；换地方

advance account 预付款账户 / advance deposit 定金，预存款 / advance money 预付账款

adverse balance of payment 支付逆差 / adverse trade balance 贸易逆差

affect calmness 假装镇静的样子

superior in quality.
